## Instructions for Givebutter sign-up

The Givebutter system helps families participate in PTA events. You can sign up for events in advance, donate to the Annual Appeal, and make other school-related purchases. Givebutter also handles our email system and SMS message system (coming soon!). Since Givebutter is used for sending out email blasts from the PTA, **make sure your contact information is correct, and your child's grade/class is updated for the current school year - Email <a href="mailto:info@mnspta.org">info@mnspta.org</a> for more information about this.**

### Don't know your Givebutter login information?

If you were an MNS family last year and have been receiving emails from the PTA, you already have a Givebutter account!

If you are a new MNS family, or need to update your contact information, go to the **<a href="https://forms.gle/k2op7o1VWDHuhaFy7">Parent Contact Form</a>** and enter your name and contact info together with your child's grade.
